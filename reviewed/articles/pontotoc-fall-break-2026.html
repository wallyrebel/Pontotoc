Pontotoc families planning the week of Oct. 12 should check which school district their children attend: the county and city calendars list different fall-break dates for 2026. County students and assistants are on break Oct. 9–16, while the city school calendar marks Oct. 12–16 as fall-break school holidays.

Pontotoc County School District separates its student and staff schedules. Its October calendar lists fall break for students and assistants from Friday, Oct. 9, through Friday, Oct. 16. Teachers have a professional-development day Oct. 9 and fall break Oct. 12–16.

Pontotoc City School District’s 2026–27 school calendar marks Oct. 12–16 as school holidays for fall break. It also marks Monday, Oct. 19, as a professional-development day with no students, so families should account for that additional day when planning.
