Pontotoc families have two football dates and state swimming to plan around during the week of Oct. 12–17, according to the weekly schedule Pontotoc Athletics posted Sunday. The lineup includes junior-high and junior-varsity football against Amory on Tuesday, a home varsity game against Caledonia on Thursday and state swimming on Saturday.

Tuesday, Oct. 13, the school’s graphic lists JH/JV football against Amory at 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30 p.m. It does not assign those times to specific grades or teams. Amory’s official middle-school schedule also lists an Oct. 13 game at Pontotoc with a 5:30 p.m. start, corroborating the date, location and first listed time.

The varsity Warriors host Caledonia on Thursday, Oct. 15, at 7 p.m. Pontotoc’s official 2026–27 varsity football schedule identifies the matchup as a home game in Pontotoc, matching the date and time on the new weekly graphic.

Saturday, Oct. 17, is listed for state swimming. The Mississippi High School Activities Association places its Class I and Class II championships that day at the Tupelo Aquatic Center. The association’s published session times differ from those on the school’s weekly graphic, so families should verify their session and arrival times with the team before traveling.

The Pontotoc Athletics graphic lists no events for Monday, Oct. 12, Wednesday, Oct. 14, or Friday, Oct. 16. Those blank days apply to this school schedule; they do not mean there are no other sports or community events around Pontotoc County. Families can use the linked official schedules to check for updates during the week.
